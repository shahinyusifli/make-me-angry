Make Him Angry

He’s walking home. Play tricks on him and make him lose his temper before he reaches his door. Don’t worry, he says sorry fast.

  • 🍌Banana peel. Drop it on the sidewalk ahead of him, but not right under his nose.
  • 🎈Water balloon. Drop it from above. Aim ahead of him, it takes a moment to fall.
  • 🐦Seagull. Sends a seagull over his head. You can guess what happens.
  • 📱Fake call. His phone rings and nobody is there. He stops to answer.
  • 👟Untie laces. Only works while he’s standing still. He trips when he walks off.

Two tricks landing within a couple of seconds make a combo. The same trick again works less well. Too many tricks too fast and he turns around: get caught and he calms down.
