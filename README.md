# Make Him Angry

A prank game. He walks home and you play tricks on him. Make him lose his temper before he reaches his door.

It is one `index.html` file with the face built in. Open it in a browser to play.

It opens with a short film. They meet on the street and he complains about everything (the sidewalk is too sidewalky, a seagull is judging his shoes, the clouds are grey on purpose). She explains, he listens, calms down, says sorry and thanks her for being patient (and compliments the bow in her hair): he gets angry fast, but he has a good heart. He walks away in a good mood, and she pulls out a banana, a water balloon and a phone to prepare her little game. Press **Skip intro**, Esc, Enter or Space to skip it, and **Watch the intro** on the start screen to see it again.

## Tricks

| Key | Trick | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Banana peel | Click the sidewalk ahead of him. Too close and he steps over it. |
| 2 | Water balloon | Click above the street to drop it. Aim a little ahead of him. |
| 3 | Seagull | A seagull flies over and hits his hair. |
| 4 | Fake call | His phone rings, nobody is there, and he stops to answer. |
| 5 | Untie laces | Only while he is standing still. He trips when he walks off. |

- **Combos:** two tricks that land within about 2.5 seconds count extra.
- **Repeats:** the same trick works less well each time.
- **Suspicion:** too many tricks too fast and he turns around. A trick used while he is looking is caught and calms him down.
- **Calming down:** he cools off slowly while he walks.

## Publish on GitHub Pages

1. Create a repository, upload `index.html` and `README.md`.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, `main`, `/ (root)`, and **Save**.
3. Play at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

The face photo is embedded in `index.html`, so anyone with the link can see it once it is public.
